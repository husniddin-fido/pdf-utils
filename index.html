Privacy Policy

PDF Studio: Edit & Sign

Effective Date: October 16, 2026

1. 100% Offline Processing & Complete Privacy

We respect your privacy above all else. PDF Studio: Edit & Sign is designed from the ground up as a privacy-first, on-device utility:

  • No Server Uploads: All document viewing, editing, page reordering, merging, splitting, compressing, annotating, and signatures are performed strictly on your local device.
  • Your files, photos, and signatures are never transmitted to our servers or any third-party cloud.

2. Storage and Device Permissions

The app accesses files solely via the official Android Storage Access Framework (SAF) and MediaStore:

  • We only read files that you explicitly choose to open.
  • We only write the files you create: to the app's own Download/PDF Utils folder, or to a location you select.

3. In-App Purchases and Financial Data

PDF Studio: Edit & Sign does not collect payments or financial information. All document utilities are available without requiring credit card or payment information.

4. Crash Reports, Usage Analytics and Notifications

To find and fix problems and to improve the app, PDF Studio uses these Google Firebase services:

  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash and error reports: what went wrong in the app's code, the device model, Android version and app version.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — usage statistics: which tools and screens are used, and whether operations succeeded.
  • Firebase Performance Monitoring — how long the app takes to start, to show screens and to complete PDF operations.
  • Firebase Remote Config — downloads settings that switch app features on or off.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging — if you allow notifications, occasional news and tips about the app.

This data never includes your documents, their file names or contents, your images or your signatures. Firebase uses an identifier for the app installation and may infer your approximate region from your IP address. The advertising ID is not collected, the app shows no ads, and the data is not sold or used for advertising.

You can turn off crash reports, analytics and performance data at any time in Settings → Share crash reports and usage data; notifications can be turned off in Android's settings. Google processes this data under the Firebase privacy and security terms.

5. Advertising

PDF Studio does not contain ad networks or data brokers. We do not sell or monetize your personal information.
